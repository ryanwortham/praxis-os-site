About Praxis

Built for owners who need a guide, not another vendor.

Praxis exists for the moment when the business has enough motion to be complicated, but not enough structure to keep the right things moving without constant force.

The partnership model

Business diagnosis and infrastructure have to stay connected.

Praxis is strongest when the business terrain and the technical build are not treated as separate worlds. The diagnosis has to shape the system. The system has to hold up after the demo.

Operational split

  • One lane owns business diagnosis, doctrine, relationship, and fit.
  • One lane owns infrastructure, implementation, reliability, and technical reality.
  • When one person owns both, something usually gets thin.
