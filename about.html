About Praxis

Built for owners who need a guide, not another vendor.

Praxis exists for the moment when a business has motion, pressure, and potential — but not enough structure to turn interest into consistent, high-impact conversations.

Founder note

Why Joel built Praxis.

Praxis is built from a simple conviction: business should create more life, not drain it. Too many owners and teams are carrying too much in their heads, wasting energy on low-value friction, and trying to grow without a clear path.

AI can help — not by replacing the human parts of business, but by protecting them. The right system can qualify, clarify, route, and prepare the conversation so humans can show up where they matter most.

“AI should not replace the human moment. It should make the human moment more prepared, more valuable, and more likely to happen.”
The partnership model

Business diagnosis and infrastructure have to stay connected.

Praxis is strongest when the business terrain and the technical build are not treated as separate worlds. The diagnosis has to shape the system. The system has to hold up after the demo.

Operational split

  • One lane owns business diagnosis, doctrine, relationship, and fit.
  • One lane owns infrastructure, implementation, reliability, and technical reality.
  • The client keeps ownership; Praxis guides the ascent.
