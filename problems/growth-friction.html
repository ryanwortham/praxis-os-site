Pressure Points / Growth Friction

When growth starts breaking the old way of operating

Praxis helps growing owner-led companies identify growth bottlenecks, operating-system gaps, support friction, and readiness issues before they compound.

Start with this problemExplore all pressure points
This usually sounds like

The symptom is familiar. The system underneath needs mapped.

  • What used to work is not working at this size.
  • More volume creates more exceptions.
  • Standards vary by person or location.
  • New people are hard to train consistently.
  • Growth is making the owner busier instead of freer.

What may be happening underneath

  • informal systems are overloaded
  • standards are not documented or adopted
  • support paths are unclear
  • decision cadence has not matured
  • training depends on proximity to the owner
  • tools are not matched to the scale of work
What Praxis maps first

Before choosing software, clarify the job.

  • what broke as volume increased
  • which pressure is people, process, tool, or training
  • where standards vary
  • what the owner still carries
  • what must become repeatable before the next growth step
  • what rhythm keeps the system honest

Possible next moves

  • growth-readiness map
  • standards and training system
  • support escalation path
  • scorecard/meeting rhythm
  • multi-location or team operating playbook
  • tool/process cleanup
Tool posture

Sometimes the fix includes AI. Sometimes it does not.

Praxis starts with the pressure point, then decides whether the answer is a better process, clearer handoff, training layer, inbound system, CRM/automation workflow, Bitta education experience, or human implementation support.
