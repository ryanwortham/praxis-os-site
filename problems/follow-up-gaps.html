Pressure Points / Follow-Up Gaps

When good opportunities fall through the cracks

Praxis helps businesses fix lead follow-up gaps, unclear ownership, missed handoffs, and response workflows that quietly leak revenue.

Start with this problemExplore all pressure points
This usually sounds like

The symptom is familiar. The system underneath needs mapped.

  • We get leads, but follow-up is inconsistent.
  • I do not know who owns the next step.
  • Quotes and estimates get sent, then nothing happens.
  • Customers ask twice because nobody closed the loop.
  • The CRM exists, but the rhythm does not.

What may be happening underneath

  • unclear ownership
  • no response-time standard
  • handoffs between people or tools are weak
  • lead context is incomplete
  • follow-up depends on memory or motivation
  • no escalation when the next step stalls
What Praxis maps first

Before choosing software, clarify the job.

  • where the lead or task enters
  • who owns each next step
  • what information is missing
  • what tool currently holds the record
  • where notification or accountability breaks
  • what follow-up should happen automatically vs personally

Possible next moves

  • lead path map
  • response-time standard
  • CRM or inbox cleanup
  • notification and reminder workflow
  • human handoff language
  • follow-up scorecard
Tool posture

Sometimes the fix includes AI. Sometimes it does not.

Praxis starts with the pressure point, then decides whether the answer is a better process, clearer handoff, training layer, inbound system, CRM/automation workflow, Bitta education experience, or human implementation support.
