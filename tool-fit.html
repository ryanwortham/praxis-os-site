Tool Fit

We start with the problem. Then we choose the tool.

Praxis is not loyal to one AI tool, CRM, form, or automation platform. Praxis is loyal to the business problem and the implementation path that can actually work.

Tool roles

Different jobs need different layers.

Bitta

Best used for education, presentation, voice-led walkthroughs, guided explanation, training, and helping people understand a complex idea.

Fin / inbound systems

Better fit when the job is lead capture, notification, routing, support flow, customer-service workflow, CRM/helpdesk handoff, or live inbound processing.

CRM and automation

Useful when the issue is follow-up discipline, reminders, ownership, routing, status visibility, or repeated administrative work.

Custom workflow

Useful when off-the-shelf tools cannot match the operating reality or when systems need a clean bridge.

Human review

Required where judgment, trust, exceptions, tradeoffs, or relationship context matter.

No software yet

Sometimes the right first move is clarifying the process, decision rights, or handoff before adding another tool.

Diagnostic

Before buying another AI tool, define the job it needs to do.

Praxis maps whether the business needs capture, notification, routing, education, training, summaries, workflow automation, decision support, or a human-first process redesign.

Questions we ask

  • What problem keeps getting worked around?
  • Who owns the next step today?
  • What information gets lost?
  • What system already holds the truth?
  • Where must human judgment stay?
  • What should not be automated yet?
Core principle

AI is a tool. Judgment is the work.

A tool can help once the job is clear. Praxis keeps the business problem and owner judgment in front, then implements side by side until the fix is real enough to use.
